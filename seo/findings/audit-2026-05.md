# SEO Audit — idealinsulationinc.com (May 2026)

## Snapshot

- **Business**: Ideal Insulation Enterprises Inc — insulation contractor (spray foam, blown-in, batt), Naples HQ, since 2013.
- **Goal**: rank #1 in Google for high-intent local searches across Collier, Lee, and Charlotte counties.
- **Site platform**: HubSpot CMS (account 243144094, US/Eastern, Marketing Hub Starter).
- **Current top-10 organic position** for `spray foam insulation Naples FL`: ~#5.
- **Current top-10 organic position** for `attic insulation Fort Myers FL contractor`: ~#6 — and the page Google chose is a *blog post*, not a service page (clear gap to close).

This audit was assembled from public Google SERP data, citation directories, and HubSpot CRM metadata. No HubSpot Private App or Google Search Console credentials were used — those will deepen the audit when provided. Findings below are sequenced by impact, not chronology.

## P0 — Bleeding right now (fix this week)

### 1. Live typo visible in Google: "Rockkwool"

`https://www.idealinsulationinc.com/blown-in-insulation-naples-swfl/` is currently showing this title in Google's SERP:

> Insulation Company Naples FL | Spray Foam Batts & Blown in **Rockkwool**

"Rockkwool" should be "Rockwool" (one k). This is the first impression Google delivers for that page. Fix today.

**Action**: in HubSpot Pages, open `blown-in-insulation-naples-swfl`, edit Page Title (Settings → Title and Meta Description), use the recommended rewrite from `seo/rewrites/page-rewrites.csv`, republish.

**Action**: walk every directory in `seo/findings/citation-runbook.md` and update or delete to match. Estimated effort: 4–6 hours for the first pass, then 30 min/month maintenance.

### 3. Yellow Pages duplicates

Three live YP listings for the same business. Google reads duplicates as a trust signal that the business identity is unclear. Pick one canonical URL, redirect the other two via YP's merge form, and add the keeper to `same_as` in `local-business.json`.

### 4. Blog index page title is `Idealinsulationinc`

The blog index — a high-traffic entry point — currently has the page title `Idealinsulationinc` (no spaces, no keywords). This wastes the most valuable signal Google reads. Fix in HubSpot Settings → Website → Blog → set the listing-page title.

## P1 — High-leverage on-page fixes (next 2–3 weeks)

### 5. Homepage title is 76 characters — Google truncates ~60

Current: `Insulation Company Naples FL | #1 Contractor Since 2013 | Ideal Insulation` (76 chars).

Recommended: `Naples Insulation Contractor | FPL Rebates | Ideal Insulation` (61 chars).

Why this version: leads with "Naples" + "Insulation Contractor" (the actual high-volume query), surfaces the "FPL Rebates" differentiator that no top-10 competitor leads with, ends with the brand. "Since 2013" is dropped from the title and moved to the meta description.

### 6. Service-page coverage gap (huge missed opportunity)

Homepage promises insulation services in **Naples, Fort Myers, Cape Coral, Bonita Springs, Marco Island, Sanibel, Estero, Lehigh Acres, Punta Gorda, and Ave Maria**. The site has dedicated service pages for **Naples only**. Every other city query is being served by either a blog post or a generic page.

For `attic insulation Fort Myers FL contractor`, Google currently ranks Ideal's *blog post* at ~#6. There is no service page to compete with the dedicated Fort Myers service pages of competitors like Igloo Insulation (#1), American Insulation (#2), or Tamiami (#3).

**Action**: build 8 service-by-city pages off one HubSpot template. Spec is in `seo/templates/service-city-template.md`. Page titles, metas, and schema are already drafted in `seo/rewrites/page-rewrites.csv` (rows tagged `P3 NEW`).

### 7. Keyword cannibalization on Naples spray foam / blown-in

Both `/` (homepage) and `/blown-in-insulation-naples-swfl/` open their `<title>` with "Insulation Company Naples FL". Google can't tell which page deserves to rank for that query, so it splits authority and ranks neither well.

**Fix**: differentiate the second title to the specific service intent — `Blown-In Attic Insulation Naples FL | Cellulose & Rockwool` — and let the homepage own the broad "insulation contractor Naples FL" query.

### 8. Bloated, keyword-stuffed URL slugs

`/professional-insulation-removal-naples-fl-emergency-service-ideal-naples-swfl` is 70+ characters and reads as spammy. Shorten to `/services/insulation-removal-naples` and 301 the old URL.

The `/services/` namespace is also healthier IA than top-level service pages. Migrate over time — set 301 redirects in HubSpot (Settings → Website → Domains & URLs → URL Redirects).

### 9. Blog URL prefix is 38 characters of overhead

`/ideal-insulation-enterprises-inc-blog/` prefix wastes URL keyword density on every post. Move to `/blog/`. Wholesale URL change with 301 redirects from old to new — this is a few hours of HubSpot config plus updating internal links.

### 10. No structured data visible in the SERP fingerprint

The current SERP has no AggregateRating stars, no FAQ accordions, no breadcrumbs from this site. These are layups. Paste-ready JSON-LD is in `seo/jsonld/`:

- `local-business.json` → homepage and `/areas-served-swfl`
- `service-spray-foam.json`, `service-blown-in.json`, `service-attic.json`, `service-removal.json` → respective service pages
- `faq-spray-foam.json` → spray foam Naples page
- `breadcrumb-template.json` → every non-homepage page (customize per page)

**How to add in HubSpot Starter**: Page editor → Settings → Advanced Options → Head HTML. Paste the JSON-LD wrapped in `<script type="application/ld+json">…</script>` tags.
